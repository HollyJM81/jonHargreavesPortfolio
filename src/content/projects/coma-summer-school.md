I conducted the World Premiere of Elif Karlidag’s work *Chorea* with the massed ensemble of the CoMA Summer School at the University of Keele in August 2025, working with my co-director Lore Lixenberg. It was an honour to be amongst the amazing array of tutors at the amazing Summer School, and I have many memories to cherish.
