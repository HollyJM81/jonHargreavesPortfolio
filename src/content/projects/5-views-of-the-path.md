On 16th June 2022, the Octandre Ensemble with Jessica Aszodi (voice), conducted by Frank Denyer, gave the premiere of his '5 Views of the Path', at the London Contemporary Music Festival. '5 Views...' explores microtonality and fragile sounds, scored for voice, three baroque flutes, genggong two percussion (stones, marbles, clothboard, knock, rattles, plant pots), mandolin and violin, The commission was generously supported by the Ernst von Siemens MusikStiftung.
