Octandre Ensemble’s first collaboration with composer Jack Sheen was a world-premiere video recording of his installation piece, *Sub*, at the Menuhin Hall, Surrey. The 50-minute piece features five alto flutes, four violas, two bass clarinets, two trombones, piano, percussion and electronics.
