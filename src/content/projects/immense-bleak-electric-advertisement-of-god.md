Octandre Ensemble workshopped composer Patrick John Jones’s ‘Immense Bleak Electric Advertisement of God’, inspired by lunar vintage photography, scored for sinfonietta. The project marked the culmination of his Leverhulme Research Fellowship at the Guildhall School of Music and Drama (London).
