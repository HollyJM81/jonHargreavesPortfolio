I was honoured to conduct the Octandre Ensemble in four Modules from Sinan Savaskan’s epic cycle, *Many Stares after semi-nocturnal Zeiss-blink*. The sinfonietta album will be release don the Métier label in 2026. We are grateful to the Vaughan Williams Foundation, the PRS Foundation, the Hinrichsen Foundation and private donors for their generous support.
