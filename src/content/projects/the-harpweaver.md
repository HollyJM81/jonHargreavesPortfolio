*'Along came Gilchrist with her own composition, The Harpweaver, a feast of melancholic optimism propelled by her febrile dexterity, flashes of wild glissandi and, in Jon Hargreaves’ and Pippa Murphy’s orchestral arrangement, poignant moments of impressionistic reflection’*  

Ken Walton, The Scotsman

*'The Celtic Connections show was opened by the SCO, under conductor Eric Jacobsen, playing Mendelssohn's Hebrides Overture, before accompanying Maeve Gilchrist, the innovative Edinburgh-born, Brooklyn-based harpist, on The Harpweaver, from her most recent album. The luminous sound produced by the orchestra and Gilchrist's Celtic, or lever, harp was worth the price of admission alone**.'*  

Russell Leadbetter, Scotland Herald

Working with Pippa Murphy, I arranged some songs from the amazing New York harpist Maeve Gilchrist’s album 'The Harpweaver’, to be played by the Scottish Chamber Orchestra at the Celtic Connections festival.
