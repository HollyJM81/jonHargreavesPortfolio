The Californian guitar and violin duo Fire and Grace asked me to make an octet arrangement of Richard Thompson’s song, ‘Beeswing’ for their appearance at the Carmel Bach Festival 2024, featuring Maeve Gilchrist. They also asked me to arrange the classic song, 'Danny Boy’. The arrangements will be perfumed as part of their concert ‘Across the Sea - A Celtic Journey’, bringing together baroque and trad music.
