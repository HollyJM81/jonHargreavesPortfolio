Under Prof. Michael Rofe, teams of technologists, composers and performers worked together, developing three real-time networked pieces of music. The final performance was held in July 2015 with a string orchestra and choir in Truro cathedral, a flute choir on the Isles of Scilly, a brass band at Lizard Point, and myself conducting from the Academy of Music and Theatre Arts, at the University of Falmouth.
