Nevis Ensemble undertook many activities during the pandemic. Our recording of *Auld Lang Syne* featured people from almost every country in the world. Our ten *Lochan Sketch* commissions featured weekly on BBC Radio Scotland, and Alex Ho’s *Breathe and Draw*, a Nevis commission, was nominated for a Scottish New Music Award.
