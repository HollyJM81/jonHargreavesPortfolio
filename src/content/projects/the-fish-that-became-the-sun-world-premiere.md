*Huddersfield Contemporary Music Festival*  

With a host of collaborators, Octandre Ensemble gave the World Premiere of Frank Denyer’s epic *The Fish that Became the Sun* (1991/96). Waiting nearly 30 years for its premiere, the piece has a legendary status in British new-music circles.
