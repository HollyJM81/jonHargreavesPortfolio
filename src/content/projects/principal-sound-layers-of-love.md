The Octande Ensemble gave a three-part concert at the Principal Sound Festival, at St. John’s, Smith Square, London. The concert included many UK Premieres, including Christian Mason’s *Layers of Love*, Claude Vivier’s Samarkand, and Savaskan’s Green Plastic Vase.
