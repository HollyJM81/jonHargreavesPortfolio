The Octandre Ensemble performed an epic, 3-part concert of works by our Composer-in-Residence, Sinan Savaskan. We commissioned *Many Stares in Semi-Nocturnal Zeiss Blink: Module 30*, which later won the BASCA National Composers Award for Large-Scale Chamber Ensemble, in 2015.
