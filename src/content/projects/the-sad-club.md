For the 2024 Connections Festival at the National Theatre, I was supporting Musical Director for the Spotlights Youth Theatre group based in Forfar, for a performance of 'The Sad Club’ in the Dorfmann Theatre. I worked alongside the inspirational group leaders Marley Laurie and Colin Grant (MD), I worked with NT Associate Director Joseph Hancock.
