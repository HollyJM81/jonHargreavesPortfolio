I brought together the material I composed and recorded for Chris Helme’s ‘World of my Own’ album to make my own album, ’Sunrise 545’. The album comprises the segues both on their own, as little miniatures, but also in the context of Chris’s songs, to showcase the sophistication of what I have done, musically. The artwork, of a piece wit the original album, is by The Seasick Sailor.

Frederick Bernas cut together some old public domain sci-fi films to make little micro-movies to publicise the release. So I have ended up with ‘Films from the Music', instead of ‘Music from the Film’.
